# User guide (outline)

Status: OUTLINE. Sections marked "after Phase N" describe features not yet built. Do not give this guide to users before the phase is done.

## 1. Logging in (after Phase 2)
- Open the site, enter your email and password.
- After 5 wrong attempts you are locked out for 15 minutes.
- Use "Log out" when you leave a shared computer.

## 2. Choosing a company (after Phase 2)
- If you belong to more than one company, pick one from the company switcher. All data you see belongs to that company only.

## 3. Team and roles (after Phase 2)
- Company Admins invite people by email and choose a role.
- Deactivate a person to remove their access immediately.

## 4. Clients and jobs (after Phase 3)
- Add a client, then create a job order for that client.
- Job status: Draft, Open, On hold, Closed.

## 5. Candidates and pipeline (after Phase 3)
- Add a candidate and attach them to a job.
- Move the candidate to the next stage. The history is kept.
- Your company decides which stages exist, including overseas stages such as Medical, Visa, Embassy, and Deployment.

## 6. Documents (after Phase 4)
- Upload PDF, JPG, or PNG files only, up to the size limit shown on screen.
- Review each document as Verified or Rejected.

## 7. CV Builder (after Phase 4)
- Choose a template. Missing information is shown as "missing". Fill it in from the candidate record.
- Reorder or hide sections. Your changes do not overwrite the candidate record.
- Review the CV, confirm it, then export the PDF.

## 8. Exports (after Phase 5)
- Export a CSV of candidates you are permitted to export. Every export is recorded.

## 9. Activity and audit (after Phase 2)
- Company Admins can see who did what in their company.

## Common questions
- "Item not found" can mean the item does not exist, or it belongs to another company. Both are shown the same way on purpose.
- Archived candidates are hidden. Ask your Company Admin to show archived records.
